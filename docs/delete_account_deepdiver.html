Account & Data Deletion — Deep Diver

Developer: Mobylex  ·  Last updated: August 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Deep Diver account and the data associated with it. Deep Diver does not use app-specific usernames or passwords — accounts are created and signed into through Google Play Games or Sign in with Apple.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email us from the account you signed in with. Send a request to the address below from the Google or Apple email you used inside Deep Diver, so we can confirm it's really you.
  2. Include your in-game display name. Add the name shown on the Leaderboard or Settings screen, and which sign-in method you used (Google Play Games or Apple) — this is how we locate your record.
  3. We confirm once it's done. Your account and saved progress are deleted within 30 days, and we'll reply to confirm.
Deletion requests: varliali@gmail.com

What Happens to Your Data

DeletedNot deleted by us
  • Your linked Google Play Games or Apple sign-in association
  • Cloud-saved progress — coins, upgrades, and owned skins
  • Daily quest progress and best-depth record
  • Your leaderboard entry (display name and score)
  • Purchase records held by Google Play or Apple's own billing systems, per their retention rules
  • Anything we're legally required to keep (e.g. for fraud prevention), for up to 30 days before final erasure

Deep Diver has no server-side account database of its own outside Unity Gaming Services — once your Cloud Save record and leaderboard entry are removed, no gameplay data tied to you remains.
