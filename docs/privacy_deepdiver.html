Privacy Policy — Deep Diver

Developer: Mobylex  ·  Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Deep Diver ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App. Deep Diver is a mobile arcade game developed by Mobylex. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Deep Diver does not use an app-specific username or password. Instead, it relies on the following to identify your account and save your progress:

CategoryWhat it isWhy we collect it
Account identifiers A Unity Player ID created automatically on first launch, and — if you choose to sign in — your linked Google Play Games or Apple ID identifier. To authenticate you and keep your progress in sync across sessions and devices.
Gameplay data Coins, owned/unlocked skins, upgrade levels, and daily quest progress. To save and restore your progress via cloud save.
Leaderboard data Your in-game display name and best depth score. To show the public leaderboard inside the App.
Purchase history Which in-app products (coin packs, premium skins) you have purchased. To deliver and restore the items you bought. We never see or store your payment card details — all payment processing is handled directly by Google Play Billing.
Device / installation ID A device-linked identifier used for anonymous authentication. To create your account before you optionally link a Google or Apple sign-in.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone/camera input, browsing history, or health data. Deep Diver does not contain chat, messaging, or any way to exchange content directly with other users.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following service providers to operate its core features. They process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own independent purposes:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Leaderboards, Cloud Code) — Unity Technologies. Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Games Services — used only if you choose to sign in with Google. Google Privacy Policy
  • Sign in with Apple — used only if you choose to sign in with Apple. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — processes all in-app purchases. Google Privacy Policy

Deep Diver does not use advertising networks, analytics/attribution SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing or advertising purposes. Your leaderboard display name and score are visible to other players inside the App as part of its core functionality, but no other personal data is made public.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your account data is retained for as long as you use the App. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see our Account & Data Deletion page for instructions. Requests are processed within 30 days. Some purchase records may be retained by Google Play independently of us, per Google's own retention rules.

Data Security

We rely on the security measures provided by Unity Gaming Services and Google Play Billing to protect your data in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Deep Diver is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
