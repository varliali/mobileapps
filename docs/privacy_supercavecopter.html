Privacy Policy — Super Cave Copter

Developer: Mobylex  ·  Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Super Cave Copter ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App. Super Cave Copter is a mobile arcade game developed by Mobylex. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Super Cave Copter has no sign-up and no passwords. The App creates an anonymous online player for the leaderboards (see below). We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other contact details inside the App.

CategoryWhat it isWhere it is kept / why
Game progress Your best score, this week's and today's (daily cave) best scores, the number of games played, the helicopter you chose in the hangar and which hints you have already seen. Stored only on your device, to show your results. It is never sent to us.
Settings Sound, music, vibration, language and usage-statistics choices, and when the "rate this game" question was last shown. Stored only on your device, to remember your preferences. It is never sent to us.
Leaderboards A random player ID, the pilot name shown on the leaderboards (a generated name such as "Pilot1234" unless you change it) and your best scores (all time, this week and today's daily cave). Stored by Unity Gaming Services, to show the leaderboards. Only your pilot name and scores are visible to other players. You can delete it with Ranks → Delete online account.
Advertising identifiers Your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA) and related ad-request data, collected by Google AdMob. On iOS, the IDFA is only available if you allow tracking when asked. To show and measure ads: occasional short ads between games, and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to keep flying once after a crash.
Ad consent choices Your answers to the privacy/consent message shown by Google's User Messaging Platform (shown to users in the EEA, the UK and other regions where required). To respect your choices about personalized ads. Stored on your device by Google's SDK.
Usage statistics Game events without personal details: a game started or ended (mode, score, how long it lasted, what ended it), the "keep flying" choice, an ad shown, the "Remove Ads" purchase and the helicopter chosen, with the random player ID, device model, operating system, app version, language and country (from the network, not your location). No advertising identifier. Sent to Unity Analytics, to see where the game is too hard or too easy and improve it. Only when your consent choices allow it (where consent is required), and you can turn it off at any time with Settings → Usage statistics.
Crash reports When the App crashes or stops responding: the error and where in the App it happened, the device model, operating system and app version. Sent to Unity Diagnostics, to find and fix the problem.
Purchase records Whether you bought "Remove Ads", and the store's receipt for it. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. To turn off ads on your device, and to restore the purchase on a new device. Stored on your device and by Apple / Google.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone/camera input, browsing history, or health data. Super Cave Copter does not contain chat, messaging or notifications.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following service providers to operate its features. They process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own independent purposes, except as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform — show ads and collect consent for personalized ads where required. How Google uses advertising data
  • Apple App Store — shows the rating dialog and handles the "Remove Ads" purchase on iPhone and iPad. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play In-App Review and Google Play Billing — show the rating dialog and handle the "Remove Ads" purchase on Android. Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Leaderboards, Analytics and Diagnostics) — the anonymous online player, the leaderboards, the usage statistics and the crash reports described above. Unity Privacy Policy

Super Cave Copter does not use attribution SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with data brokers. Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to select, deliver, and measure ads, and to help prevent the same ad from repeating; this is governed by Google's own privacy policy linked above.

Optional Actions That Leave the App

  • Share Game opens your device's share screen. What you share and with whom is up to you; we do not receive it.
  • Rate Us and the rating question open the App Store / Google Play review screen, which is handled by Apple or Google.
  • More Games opens our developer page on the App Store / Google Play.
  • Feedback: if you decline the rating question, the App may offer a feedback form that opens in your browser. Anything you write there is sent only if you submit the form, and is used only to improve the game.

Your Choices

  • Personalized ads: where required, you can change your consent at any time with Settings → Privacy Options in the App.
  • iOS tracking: you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's Google / Privacy → Ads settings.
  • Remove Ads: an optional one-time purchase that turns off ads between games and lets you keep flying without watching a video. After it, the App requests no more ads.
  • Usage statistics: turn them off at any time with Settings → Usage statistics.
  • Online account: change your pilot name or delete your online account in Ranks.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google AdMob and the leaderboard data handled by Unity as described above, no data leaves your device.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because your game progress and settings are stored only on your device, uninstalling the App deletes them. We do not keep a copy. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its privacy policy linked above.

Deleting your online account (Ranks → Delete online account) removes your player and pilot name; scores already on the leaderboards may remain under the generic name "Pilot", no longer tied to an account.

Data Security

The App communicates with Google AdMob, Unity Gaming Services and the app stores over encrypted connections (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Super Cave Copter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
