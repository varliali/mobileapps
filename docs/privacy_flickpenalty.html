Privacy Policy — Flick Penalty

Developer: Mobylex  ·  Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Flick Penalty ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App. Flick Penalty is a mobile football game developed by Mobylex. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Flick Penalty has no user accounts of its own. We do not ask for your email address, real name or any other contact details inside the App. Story Mode and Shoot Frenzy work entirely on your device; only the Online mode, the gameplay statistics described below and the services you use (ads, purchases, achievements) send data off your device.

CategoryWhat it isWhere it is kept / why
Game progress Your Story levels, stars and lives, your best Shoot Frenzy score, your coins and training levels, the balls, trails and kits you own and use, your daily gift and streak, your match statistics and which achievements you have unlocked. Stored only on your device, to continue your game and show your results. It is never sent to us.
Settings Sound, music, language and reminder choices, whether the tutorial has been shown, and when the "rate this game" question was last shown. Stored only on your device, to remember your preferences. It is never sent to us.
Online mode When you open Online, the App signs in anonymously and receives a random player ID (no account, email or password). It stores the player name you choose (or a default such as "Player1234"), your online rating, your latest recorded kicks and dives (the direction, speed and spin of each kick and where you dived) and the look of your keeper. Kept on Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication, Cloud Save and Leaderboards) so other players can play against your recorded kicks and dives and so the rating table and monthly seasons work. Your player name, rating, rank and recorded kicks and dives are visible to other players of the App. Nothing is sent if you never open Online. Do not use your real name or other personal details as your player name.
Gameplay statistics Unity Analytics events about how the game is played: match results and scores, Shoot Frenzy scores, online match results, daily streak choices, ads shown, coins spent and purchases made, together with Unity's standard session data (an installation ID, app version, device model, operating system, language and approximate country). Sent to Unity Analytics so we can see which parts of the game work and keep the game balanced. Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, this is only collected if you agree in the privacy message (store and access information on the device, and measure content performance). It is never used for advertising.
Achievements and leaderboards Your Game Center (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play Games (Android) player profile, the achievements you unlock and your best Shoot Frenzy score. Sent to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games so you can see your achievements and compare scores with other players. Handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we do not receive it. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.
Reminders If you turn reminders on, local notifications are scheduled on your device for the evening, reminding you of your daily gift or a daily streak at risk. Scheduled by your device's own notification system. No push notification service is used, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in your device's notification settings.
Purchase history Whether you have purchased "Remove Ads" or "Unlock Levels", and the coin packs, level skips and extra lives you buy. To deliver and restore your purchases. We never see or store your payment card details — all payment processing is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play Billing.
Advertising identifiers Your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA) and related ad-request data, collected by Google AdMob. On iOS, the IDFA is only available if you allow tracking when asked. To show and measure ads: occasional short ads between matches (not shown with "Remove Ads") and optional rewarded video ads you can choose to watch, for example for free coins, a second chance in Story Mode or to keep your daily streak.
Ad and data consent choices Your answers to the privacy/consent message shown by Google's User Messaging Platform (shown to users in the EEA, the UK and other regions where required). To respect your choices about personalized ads and gameplay statistics. Stored on your device by Google's SDK.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone/camera input, browsing history, or health data. Flick Penalty does not contain chat or messaging; other players only see the player name you choose.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following service providers to operate its features. They process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own independent purposes, except as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Leaderboards, Analytics) and Unity In-App Purchasing — the Online mode, gameplay statistics, and the library that talks to the App Store / Google Play to complete and restore purchases. Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform — show ads and collect consent where required. How Google uses advertising data
  • Apple App Store and Game Center — process in-app purchases, show the rating dialog, achievements and leaderboards on iPhone and iPad. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing, Google Play Games and Google Play In-App Review — process in-app purchases, show achievements and leaderboards and the rating dialog on Android. Google Privacy Policy

Flick Penalty does not use attribution or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not sell your personal data.

Optional Actions That Leave the App

  • Share opens your device's share screen. What you share and with whom is up to you; we do not receive it.
  • Rate Us and the rating question open the App Store / Google Play review screen, which is handled by Apple or Google.
  • More Games opens our developer page on the App Store / Google Play.
  • Feedback: if you decline the rating question, the App may offer a feedback form that opens in your browser. Anything you write there is sent only if you submit the form, and is used only to improve the game.

Your Choices

  • Personalized ads and gameplay statistics: where required, you can change your consent at any time with Settings → Privacy.
  • Online mode: optional. You can change your player name at any time on the Online page; if you never open Online, nothing is sent to Unity Gaming Services for it.
  • iOS tracking: you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's Google / Privacy → Ads settings.
  • Game Center / Play Games: you can sign out, or hide your activity, in your device's Game Center or Play Games settings.
  • Reminders: off unless you turn them on; switch them off any time in Settings → Reminders or in your device's notification settings.
  • Fewer ads: the "Remove Ads" purchase removes the ads shown between matches; rewarded ads are always optional.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google AdMob, the Online and statistics data handled by Unity Gaming Services, and the purchase, achievement and leaderboard data handled by Apple / Google, as described above, no data leaves your device. Your online player name, rating and recorded kicks and dives are shown to other players.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device, so uninstalling the App deletes them. We do not keep a copy. Online data (player ID, name, rating and recorded kicks and dives) stays on Unity Gaming Services while it is used for matches; past seasons' rating tables are archived. Uninstalling the App does not delete it, but it is no longer linked to you, as the anonymous sign-in is lost. To have your online data and gameplay statistics deleted, email us (see below) with your online player name; we will delete it within 30 days. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their own retention rules, which lets you restore "Remove Ads" and "Unlock Levels" on a new device with Restore purchases (coins and other consumable items are stored on the device and cannot be restored). Achievements and leaderboard scores are kept by Apple Game Center or Google Play Games. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its privacy policy linked above.

Data Security

The App communicates with Unity Gaming Services, Google AdMob, Game Center / Play Games and the app stores over encrypted connections (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Flick Penalty is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.
