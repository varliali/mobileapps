Privacy Policy — Brain Box Puzzle

Developer: Mobylex  ·  Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Brain Box Puzzle ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App. Brain Box Puzzle is a mobile puzzle game developed by Mobylex. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Brain Box Puzzle does not have user accounts, sign-in, cloud save, or leaderboards. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other contact details inside the App.

CategoryWhat it isWhere it is kept / why
Game progress Solved levels, best move counts and stars, the position of a level you have not finished, and your remaining hints. Stored only on your device, so you can continue where you left off. It is never sent to us.
Settings Sound, music, language and theme choices, and when the "rate this game" question was last shown. Stored only on your device, to remember your preferences. It is never sent to us.
Purchase history Which in-app products (Premium, hint packs) you have purchased. To deliver and restore the items you bought. We never see or store your payment card details — all payment processing is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play Billing.
Advertising identifiers Your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA) and related ad-request data, collected by Google AdMob. On iOS, the IDFA is only available if you allow tracking when asked. To show and measure ads: short ads between levels (not shown with Premium) and optional rewarded video ads you can choose to watch for a free hint.
Ad consent choices Your answers to the privacy/consent message shown by Google's User Messaging Platform (shown to users in the EEA, the UK and other regions where required). To respect your choices about personalized ads. Stored on your device by Google's SDK.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone/camera input, browsing history, or health data. Brain Box Puzzle does not contain chat, messaging, or any way to exchange content with other users.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following service providers to operate its features. They process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own independent purposes, except as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform — show ads and collect consent for personalized ads where required. How Google uses advertising data
  • Apple App Store — processes in-app purchases on iPhone and iPad. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases on Android. Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity In-App Purchasing — the library inside the App that talks to the App Store / Google Play to complete and restore purchases. Unity Privacy Policy

Brain Box Puzzle does not use analytics/attribution SDKs or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with data brokers. Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to select, deliver, and measure ads, and to help prevent the same ad from repeating; this is governed by Google's own privacy policy linked above.

Optional Actions That Leave the App

  • Share Game opens your device's share screen. What you share and with whom is up to you; we do not receive it.
  • Rate Us and the rating question open the App Store / Google Play review screen, which is handled by Apple or Google.
  • Feedback: if you decline the rating question, the App may offer a feedback form that opens in your browser. Anything you write there is sent only if you submit the form, and is used only to improve the game.

Your Choices

  • Personalized ads: where required, you can change your consent at any time with Settings → Privacy Options in the App.
  • iOS tracking: you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's Google / Privacy → Ads settings.
  • Fewer ads: the Premium purchase removes the ads shown between levels; rewarded ads are always optional.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google AdMob and the purchase data handled by Apple / Google as described above, no data leaves your device.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because your game progress and settings are stored only on your device, uninstalling the App deletes them. We do not keep a copy. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their own retention rules, which lets you restore Premium on a new device with Shop → Restore Purchases. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its privacy policy linked above.

Data Security

The App communicates with Google AdMob and the app stores over encrypted connections (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Brain Box Puzzle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
